1 · listen
space play/pause. solo a lane to hear only that instrument; mute hides one. Slow it down with the speed menu.
2 · move around
click anywhere on a lane to jump there · ←→ one beat · shift+arrows one bar · home start · mouse wheel zooms, shift+wheel scrolls.
3 · mark a moment
press M the instant you hear it, then type what happens and what the game should do. shift+M sets where a selected mark ends. Drag a flag in the ruler to move it. [ ] jump between marks · ⌫ deletes · ⌘Z undoes.
4 · study a section
I then O set a loop around it, L turns the loop on. Red ▲ under drums = the heaviest sub-bass hits. Faint bands in the ruler = sections a machine guessed.
