## Flag 1 — the lull before turret alley (run 2, S2, stageT 753, frame 612)

**Jacob:** "after the popcorn enemy waves, there is this lull where no enemies
are on screen for what feels like too long. but when the turrets arrive, the

bored. Technically I was positioning my ship for the first turret to arrive."

**Replay:** last S1 popcorn kill at tick 494; at the flag (tick 612) both

**Coding:** experience Interested/Bored → Bored; Purposeful/Aimless → aimless
(knew what was coming, nothing to do). Object: timeline pacing, S1→S2 seam.
Positive to protect: the turret arrival lands on the music's lift.

## Flag 3 — turret alley with crossers (run 4, S2 rep 2, stageT 1305, frame 1131)

**Jacob:** "although adding the popcorn enemies to the turrets section did add
overlapping enemies, it doesn't feel cohesive. for one, all the enemies are
grey geometrical shapes of similar sizes. also the part of the screen where the
popcorn arrives doesn't feel good… the top sides doesn't feel right." Expected:
"overlapping enemies and that did happen but it does not feel much more
satisfying nor challenging." Words: Confused, Disappointed. Clarification (chat):
"There was no confusion about what shot me. It's the fact they all look so
similar — does not feel good. Everything just looks like grey, boring,
geometric shapes."

**Replay:** rep 1 was a clean sweep — every kill a SPEED kill (4 turrets, all
popcorn and crossers, chain intact); death at frame 1104 to a bullet at
(96,184), 18 ticks after a 3-bullet prong fired while a crosser was on screen
(the crosser's mid-crossing shot). At the flag: 3 turrets (one vulnerable),
1 crosser at (81,59), chain 0, lives 2.

**Coding:** experience Satisfied/Dissatisfied → Dissatisfied; Interested/Bored
→ Bored; NOT Understand/Confused (Jacob explicitly). Object: **enemy visual
identity** (primary), crosser entry position (secondary). The overlap works
mechanically (r19 did what it said); it doesn't *read* as an encounter because
nothing distinguishes the participants.

## What this means (design step, separate from the interview)

1. **Enemy identity is the cohesion problem, not the overlap.** The renderer
   draws enemies as "desaturated silhouettes, distinct per role (S4)" so that
   bullets keep top value-contrast (Pillar 6) — the cost is that popcorn,
   crossers and turrets all read as grey geometry. The canon research says the
   same thing from the other side: Komazawa — "every enemy and character in
   that game had a backstory"; DDP's scale hierarchy — "big things are slow;
   fast things are small"; ground vs air as visibly different layers. Identity
   has to come from **shape, size, motion and layer**, not saturated colour.
   Candidates: a real size hierarchy (popcorn small, turrets squat-and-wide,
   mids tall), planes vs ground vehicles as two silhouette families, crossers
   as their own shape (they behave differently — they must look different),
   shadows/anchoring for ground objects, and the G-pass scenery so the world
   under them isn't slabs. This is an art pass; it was always going to be the
   next hole after density.
2. **The S1→S2 seam has a 2.5–3 s hole.** Options: pull turret alley's base
   earlier by ~90–120 stageT, spawn the first pair already at y −4 so
   vulnerability is ~40f away, or bridge with a 3-popcorn file (DDP's "mortar
   between bricks"). Constraint: the music lift on turret arrival is loved —
   check the stage-track timing before moving the seam.
3. **Crosser entry position** — "top sides doesn't feel right" but he
   couldn't say where. Not a design question to hand back to him. Try, in the
   Booth: enter at y 90–120 (the lane between the turrets and the ship) and
   from mid-side like Garegga's side tanks; compare in play.
4. **Booth process notes:** answers in chat lose the frame stamp — Jacob will
   answer in the panel next time. Tab presses that are resumed without sending
   still increment the flag number (harmless, but the debrief should say so).
   The pad flag button (R2) shipped mid-session.
